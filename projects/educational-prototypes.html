educational game prototypes

rapid prototyping exploration of phonics learning mechanics for middle schoolers through iterative design and user research. designed and developed 5 unique educational game prototypes testing different approaches to phonemic awareness.

role: technical game designer
team: 4 members
duration: may 2024 – aug 2024
platform: pc, web,board games
tools: Unity, C#, Python, CMU pronunciation library
institution: center for transformational play, cmu
research board

key contributions

  • designed and developed 5+ educational game prototypes testing different phonics learning mechanics
  • established EOTA methodology (experience, observations, theory, advice) for structured user feedback
  • conducted weekly user research sessions with target demographic to validate design decisions
  • built modular systems in unity and web technologies for rapid iteration cycles

prototype showcase

each prototype explored different learning mechanics and interaction patterns

impact and learnings

this research project validated my ability to rapidly prototype and iterate educational game concepts while maintaining rigorous user research standards. the EOTA methodology proved valuable for structured feedback collection, and the diversity of prototypes demonstrated adaptability across different technical platforms and interaction paradigms.

the project reinforced my interest in using game design to make learning more engaging, particularly in areas where traditional educational approaches struggle to maintain student motivation.
